`containers/`. The versions below are the direct build, runtime, and validation

The fully resolved CPU environment, including transitive packages, is recorded
in `containers/requirements-cpu.lock`. The CUDA-12 file records direct
requirements only; the CUDA driver and all accelerator wheels must therefore be
resolved and recorded on the target Linux system before claiming bitwise
environment reproducibility. This limitation is also recorded in
`reports/VALIDATION_REPORT.md`.

Installed wheels may contain additional compatible libraries (for example
BLAS/LAPACK in NumPy or SciPy wheels). Their notices are distributed with those
wheels; no wheel is included in this archive.

The original supplementary implementation and accompanying original repository
material are released under the MIT License supplied in `LICENSE` and declared
in `pyproject.toml`. This licensing revision was authorized on 2026-10-05.
It does not change the licenses of any dependency listed above. Dependencies
are installed separately, with their upstream license files and notices.

No external dataset, pretrained checkpoint, or third-party software distribution
is included. Numerical tables and figure files are generated by the experiments.
This inventory is not a claim of legal clearance for any future redistribution
of installed environments or CUDA/container images; such distributions must
retain the applicable upstream notices, including bundled transitive libraries.
